Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This page says what the Little Archive hosted service and apps keep about you, who else receives any of it, and how to remove it.

Who runs it

The Little Archive hosted service and the Little Archive apps for iPhone and Android are run by Nuzio, a company in Kuwait. In this policy "we" means Nuzio.

Little Archive is also open-source software that anyone can run on their own server. This policy covers the hosted service, which is what you use when you choose "Little Archive account" in the app. If you choose "My own server", see Your own server.

What we keep

WhatDetailsWhy
Account email and sign-in Your email address and password are held by Supabase, the sign-in service we use. If you use Continue with Google, Google gives Supabase your email address and usually your name and the address of your profile picture. If you use Continue with Apple, Apple gives Supabase your email address or a private relay address. The Little Archive server does not store your email address or your password. To sign you in.
Account id A random identifier that Supabase gives your account. The Little Archive server uses it to find your library. To keep your library separate from everyone else's.
What you save Links, titles, captions, creator names, collections, tags, notes, favourites, preview images you add, files you import, details read from a saved post's public page, transcripts, recipe cards, places and AI suggestions. This is your library. Storing it is the service.
Purchase record Whether your account is unlocked and where it was bought. For an App Store purchase, Apple's transaction id. For a Google Play purchase, a scrambled form (a hash) of Google's purchase token. For a payment on the web, Tap's charge id, the amount and whether it was paid. We never receive your card or KNET details, and we do not keep the name or email address you give Tap for the receipt. To unlock your account, restore a purchase and act on a refund.
Apple sign-in token Only if you use Continue with Apple, and only once we have switched on the removal of Apple sign-ins: one token that Apple gives our server for your account. So that, when you delete your account, our server can tell Apple to end the sign-in. It is deleted at that moment.
Usage counters Counts of how many AI suggestions, closer reads and other AI requests your account has made today and this month. They are numbers only, with no text, and are stored under a hash of your account id. To apply the fair-use limits.
Network address The server holds the address your request came from in memory for a short time. The application keeps no access log unless we turn one on to investigate a problem. Our hosting provider or proxy may keep short-lived connection logs. To limit request rates and block abuse.

On your phone, the app keeps your sign-in in the system's protected storage (the iOS Keychain or the Android Keystore).

Where your library lives, and who can read it

Your library is stored on a server that Nuzio runs. Each account has its own separate database. Data travels between the app and the server over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).

Your library is not end-to-end encrypted. The people who operate the server at Nuzio are technically able to read it, and it is included in the backups we make of the server. We look at an account's data only when that is needed to keep the service running, to answer a request from the account's owner, or when the law requires it.

Who else receives data, and why

CompanyWhat it receivesWhy
Supabase Your email address, your password (which it stores in protected form) and, if you sign in with Google or Apple, what they share about you. Sign-in only. Your library is not stored at Supabase.
Google (Gemini) When you use an AI feature: the text of the saves you chose, meaning the post's title (including one you typed), creator, caption, image description and transcript. For "ask your archive": the question you typed and short passages from the saves that match it. For a closer read, if we use Gemini to transcribe: the post's media file. To suggest titles, folders and summaries, build recipe cards and places, answer your question, and turn speech into text.
Groq For a closer read: the media file of the public post, which carries its sound and, for a video, its picture. To turn the speech in the post into a transcript.
Apple If you buy the unlock on an iPhone or iPad, Apple takes the payment. If you use Continue with Apple, Apple signs you in. Payment and sign-in.
Google If you buy the unlock on Android, Google Play takes the payment, and our server asks Google whether the purchase is valid. If you use Continue with Google, Google signs you in. Payment and sign-in.
Tap Payments If you buy the unlock on the web, our server sends Tap Payments (Kuwait) the price, your account id, and the name and email address you give for the receipt, then sends your browser to Tap's own page. You pay there by KNET, Apple Pay or card. Your card or KNET details go to Tap and never reach our server. Tap tells us whether the payment succeeded. Payment.
Map and place-lookup services If the places map is switched on: the name, address, city and country of a place mentioned in a save go from our server to a place-lookup service to find it on the map, and your phone loads the map's pictures from a map service, which sees your phone's network address. Neither receives your saves, your account or your email address. To show saved places on a map.

More about the AI features:

  • They run only when you start them. Nothing in your library is sent to an AI company in the background.
  • Before the first one, the app shows what would be sent and to which service, and asks you to allow it. Nothing is sent unless you allow it; our server refuses AI work for an account that has not allowed it. Your answer is kept with your account, and you can change it in Settings. A request already on its way when you switch it off cannot be recalled.
  • Your own notes and the names of your collections are not sent to Google or Groq.
  • The media file for a closer read is fetched by our server from the public post, held in memory while it is transcribed, and then discarded. We keep the transcript, not the video or audio.
  • Once text or media has been sent to Google or Groq, their own terms decide how long they keep it. We cannot take it back.
  • An AI suggestion is a guess made from text. It is not a checked fact about a post.

When you ask the app to read a saved post's page, our server requests that public page. The site that hosts the post sees a request from our server, with no account and no cookies, not from your phone.

Loading preview pictures from other sites is off unless you turn it on in Settings. If you turn it on, your phone asks the site that hosts each picture for it, and that site sees your phone's network address.

These companies may process data in countries other than Kuwait and other than the one you live in.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising software in the apps.
  • No tracking of you across other apps or websites.
  • No analytics software in the apps.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not give it to anyone other than the services described on this page, for the purposes described.
  • The apps do not ask for your location, your contacts or your social media passwords. The iPhone app asks for the camera only if you take a photo as a preview picture.

Your own server

If you choose "My own server" in the app, or run Little Archive on your own computer, the app talks only to the server you named. Nuzio receives nothing: no account, no library and no usage counts. There is nothing to buy, and no AI company receives anything unless the person who runs that server has set one up. Whoever runs that server is responsible for its data, and this policy does not apply to it. The technical privacy notes describe what the software does on a server you run.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep your library for as long as your account exists. You can delete a single save at any time, and you can export your library or download a full backup from Settings.

You can delete your account in the app: Settings, "This device", "Delete my account…". The account deletion page has the steps and a way to ask by email. Deleting your account:

  • removes your library, your preview images, any import file still waiting, and your unlock, your purchase record and any Apple sign-in token from our server straight away;
  • then removes your sign-in from Supabase. If Supabase cannot be reached at that moment, the server keeps trying until it is gone, and holds your account id for that purpose until it is.

What stays for a limited time, none of it with your content, your email address or your account id in readable form:

  • a record of when the deletion happened, stored under a hash of your account id, for 30 days, so that an old sign-in on another device cannot bring the account back;
  • the current day's and month's usage counts, under the same hash, until that day and that month end;
  • if a store refunded or took back your purchase, a record of that purchase with no account attached, so the same purchase cannot be used again.

What deletion cannot reach:

  • exports and backups you downloaded, which are yours to keep or delete;
  • backups of the server that we made before you deleted your account, until those backups are replaced;
  • text or media already sent to Google or Groq for an AI feature;
  • records Apple, Google or Tap keep about your payment;
  • if you used Continue with Apple, the entry for Little Archive in your Apple Account may remain. Where we hold an Apple sign-in token for your account, our server asks Apple to end the sign-in when you delete the account; it cannot confirm that Apple did. You can always remove the entry yourself on your iPhone, under Settings, Apple Account, Sign in with Apple.

Children

Little Archive is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly hold an account for a child under 13. If you believe a child has made an account, write to us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect or who receives it, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.
