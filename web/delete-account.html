Delete your Little Archive account

This page is for people who have an account on the Little Archive hosted service, run by Nuzio. It explains how to delete the account and its data, with or without the app.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Little Archive and sign in.
  2. Open Settings and find the section "This device".
  3. Choose "Delete my account…".
  4. Type DELETE to confirm, then choose "Delete my account".

The deletion happens straight away and cannot be undone. If you want a copy of your library, export it or download a backup from Settings first.

Ask us to delete it

If you no longer have the app, write to the address under Contact:

  • Send the message from the email address of your Little Archive account, so we know the request is yours.
  • Put "Delete my account" in the subject.
  • If you signed up with Continue with Apple and hid your email address, say so. We may ask you to confirm the request from inside your account.

We will delete the account within 30 days of receiving your message, and reply to tell you when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your whole library: every save, collection, tag, note, transcript, recipe card, place and AI suggestion.
  • Preview images you added, and any import file still waiting.
  • Your unlock and the record of your purchase on our server, including the record of a payment made on the web.
  • The Apple sign-in token, if our server holds one for your account.
  • Your sign-in (your email address and password) at Supabase, the sign-in service we use. If Supabase cannot be reached at that moment, our server keeps trying until it is removed, and holds your account id for that purpose until it is.

What is kept, and for how long

WhatHow longWhy
The time of the deletion, stored under a hash of the account id 30 days So an old sign-in on another device cannot bring the account back.
Counts of AI requests for the current day and month, under the same hash Until that day and that month end So deleting and signing up again does not reset the fair-use limits.
A record of a purchase that the store refunded or took back, with no account attached Kept So a refunded purchase cannot be used again.
Backups of our server made before the deletion Until those backups are replaced Backups cannot be edited one account at a time.

None of the first three holds your content, your email address or your account id in readable form.

What deleting your account does not change

  • Exports and backups you downloaded stay on your own devices.
  • Text or media that was already sent to an AI company for a feature you used cannot be recalled.
  • Apple, Google or Tap keep their own record of your payment. Deleting your account does not refund a purchase.
  • If you used Continue with Apple, Little Archive may stay listed in your Apple Account. Where our server holds an Apple sign-in token for your account, it asks Apple to end the sign-in as part of the deletion; it cannot confirm that Apple did. You can always remove the entry yourself on your iPhone, under Settings, Apple Account, Sign in with Apple.
  • If you used your own server, there is no hosted account to delete. Your library is on that server.
