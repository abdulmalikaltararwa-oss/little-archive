What is free

Saving, importing, exporting, titles, folders and search are free and are not limited in number. A free account also gets a limited number of AI suggestions. Each account has a storage limit; the app tells you if you reach it.

The one-time unlock

There is one thing to buy. You pay once. It is not a subscription, it does not renew, and there are no ads and no credit packs.

The unlock adds, for your account:

  • closer reads, which make a transcript of a saved video's speech;
  • recipe cards and cook mode;
  • the places map;
  • "ask your archive";
  • a larger allowance of AI suggestions.

Fair use. These features cost us money each time they run, so an unlocked account has daily and monthly limits, set high enough for ordinary personal use. When you reach a limit the app says so and says when it starts again. Settings shows what your account has used. We may adjust the limits, but we will not lower them to a level that makes the unlock pointless for ordinary personal use.

One account. A purchase unlocks one Little Archive account, on every device where you sign in to that account. "Restore a purchase" on the unlock screen brings it back after you reinstall the app or change phone. A purchase cannot unlock a second account at the same time.

Your own server. If you run Little Archive on your own server, every feature is open and there is nothing to buy.

Refunds

  • Bought through the App Store or Google Play. The store took the payment and handles refunds under its own refund policy. Ask the store. We cannot refund a store purchase ourselves.
  • Bought on the web through Tap Payments. If you are not satisfied, write to us within 14 days of paying and we will refund the full amount to the payment method you used.

When a purchase is refunded, the unlock is removed from the account.
